The flat

Casa Maria is a two-bedroom flat in Estoril Villas, about 500 m from Diaz Beach and 150 m from Diaz Water Park. The open-plan lounge and kitchen lead onto a balcony with a built-in braai and a view down the road to the sea. Inside it is light and simple, done in blues and whites, with a smart TV in the lounge and another in the main bedroom.

    Around Diaz Beach

    Diasstrand lies between Mossel Bay town and Hartenbos, with Diaz Beach a short walk from the flat and the Langeberg Mall 5 minutes away. Santos Beach, 6 minutes’ drive, faces north into the bay and is usually calmer, and last season lifeguards were on duty there and at Hartenbos from 1 December. In town you’ll find the Dias Museum, braai food at Kaai 4 on the harbour and the cliff path from The Point.
